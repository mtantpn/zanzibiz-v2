Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: May 2025

Summary

ZanziBiz may earn a commission when you click booking or travel links on this site, at no extra cost to you. Our editorial content is independent and honest regardless of affiliate relationships.

What Is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a way for websites to earn a small commission by recommending products or services. When you click an affiliate link and complete a purchase or booking, the retailer or travel provider pays ZanziBiz a referral fee. You pay exactly the same price you would have paid anyway — sometimes even less, thanks to special rates.

Our Affiliate Relationships

ZanziBiz participates in affiliate programmes including, but not limited to:

  • Booking.com Partner Programme — hotel and accommodation bookings in Zanzibar and worldwide.
  • Travelpayouts — flights, tours, transfers, and travel insurance. This includes partner brands such as Kiwi.com (flights), Kiwitaxi and GetTransfer (airport transfers), Klook and Tiqets (tours and activities), Yesim (eSIM and mobile data), EKTA (travel insurance), and others.
  • Other travel and local service providers where relevant.

These links appear in the Zanzibar Travel Guide to help visitors plan their trip. You should always compare options and choose what suits your needs. We do not receive payment to place these links in a specific order — they are organized by what travellers typically need first.

How We Use Affiliate Links

Affiliate links may appear in:

  • Travel guide articles (e.g., "Book your ferry here" or "Find hotels in Nungwi")
  • Useful links sections within guide posts
  • Recommendations in the business directory

Affiliate links are used for services and products we genuinely believe are useful to Zanzibar travelers. We do not recommend services solely because of an affiliate relationship.

Our Editorial Independence

Affiliate relationships do not influence our travel guide content. We write honestly about destinations, businesses, and services in Zanzibar. If we have concerns about a product or service, we will say so regardless of any commercial relationship.

Questions?

If you have questions about this disclosure or our affiliate relationships, please contact us.
