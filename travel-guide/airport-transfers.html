Zanzibar Airport Transfers

Taxis, shared shuttles, and private drivers — how to get from Zanzibar Airport (ZNZ) to your hotel, with real pricing and travel times.

By ZanziBiz · Updated 28 November 2026 · Prices are approximate; check before you book.

In short

  • Taxis wait outside arrivals at Zanzibar Airport (ZNZ). They have no meters, so agree the price and the currency before you get in.
  • Approximate taxi or private transfer times and prices from the airport: Stone Town 15–20 min, $10–15. Nungwi or Kendwa 60–90 min, $35–55. Paje or Jambiani 50–70 min, $30–45. Matemwe or Kiwengwa 60–80 min, $40–60.
  • A shared shuttle costs about $10–20 per person but can add 30–60 minutes for other drop-offs.
  • For a late-night arrival, pre-book a transfer or ask your hotel. Many hotels offer airport pick-up.

Quick tip: Zanzibar Airport (ZNZ) is about 7 km south of Stone Town. There are no official metered taxis — always agree on a price before getting in the vehicle, or pre-book a transfer through your hotel or a trusted operator.

Your Transfer Options Explained

1. Standard taxi (at the airport)

Taxis wait outside arrivals at ZNZ. There are no meters — always agree the price before you get in. It helps to know the approximate rates above. Bargaining is expected and normal. Paying in USD or TZS is both accepted, but agree the currency in advance.

2. Pre-booked private transfer

The easiest option, especially arriving late at night or with heavy luggage. Ask your hotel if they offer airport pick-up (many do, sometimes included in the room rate). Alternatively, book through a local transfer company — your driver meets you in arrivals with a name board. Rates are comparable to a taxi, but with no negotiation and confirmed reliability.

3. Shared shuttle

Cheaper than a private transfer, especially if you're traveling solo. Shared shuttles run to popular areas like Nungwi and Paje. The downside is multiple drop-offs, which can add 30–60 minutes to your journey. Ask at the airport information desk or your hotel for current shuttle providers.

4. Hotel vehicle

Many mid-range and upscale hotels offer their own airport transfers, included or at a fixed charge. Always ask when booking — it often works out cheaper and more convenient than arranging separately.

Practical Tips

  • Have small USD bills ready — $10, $20 denominations are easiest for taxis.
  • If it's your first trip, consider paying the slightly higher rate for a pre-booked transfer — the peace of mind on arrival is worth it.
  • Night arrivals: taxis are still available at ZNZ around the clock, but pre-booking is more reassuring for late flights.
  • If connecting from Dar es Salaam via a short domestic hop, your bags may not always arrive on the same flight — allow time at arrivals before assuming they're lost.
  • Confirm the meeting point with your driver — ZNZ arrivals is small and easy to navigate.
