About ZanziBiz

ZanziBiz is Zanzibar's dedicated travel guide and local business directory, built to help travelers and residents discover the very best the island has to offer.

Our Mission

We believe traveling to Zanzibar should be easy, safe, and full of authentic local experiences. Our mission is to provide honest, practical travel information alongside a trusted directory of verified local businesses — from hotels and restaurants to tour operators, transport services, and beyond.

What We Cover

Our travel guides cover everything you need to plan your Zanzibar trip: where to stay in Stone Town, Nungwi, Paje, Kendwa and Jambiani; the best beaches and how to reach them; getting around by dala-dala, taxi, or scooter; where to eat; local culture and customs; safety tips; and practical information for first-time visitors and repeat travelers alike.

The Business Directory

Alongside the travel guide, ZanziBiz runs a comprehensive local business directory. Business owners can list their services to reach travelers and locals directly. Every listing goes through a basic review process to help ensure quality and accuracy.
