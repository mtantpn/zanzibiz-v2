When you land

Airport to your hotel: what a taxi costs by area

Prices checked with 4 drivers · sample text for the prototype · next check due 1 Mar 2027

Short answer

From Abeid Amani Karume airport, a taxi to Stone Town is usually TZS 25,000–35,000 and takes about 20 minutes. Nungwi and Paje are around an hour away. Agree the price before you load your bags, and ask whether it is per car or per person.

Typical fares from the airport

DestinationTimeFare (TZS, per car)
Stone Town20 min25,000–35,000
Fumba25 min35,000–45,000
Paje / Jambiani60 min70,000–90,000
Kiwengwa / Matemwe60 min70,000–90,000
Nungwi / Kendwa70 min80,000–100,000

Three things to agree before you get in

  1. The total price, and the currency. Shillings are usually cheaper than dollars.
  2. Whether a stop on the way, such as a SIM shop or cash machine, costs extra.
  3. The driver's WhatsApp, so you can book the return trip.
