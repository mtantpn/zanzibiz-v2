Where to Go

Zanzibar's key destinations

Each part of Zanzibar has a distinct character. Here's a quick breakdown to help you choose where to base yourself.

Heritage

Stone Town

UNESCO World Heritage site. Arabic architecture, spice markets, vibrant food scene.
Beaches

Nungwi

North tip of the island. White sand, calm swimming, dhow trips at sunset.
Kitesurfing

Paje & Jambiani

Southeast coast kitesurfing hub. Chill atmosphere, excellent seafood, tidal flats.
Resorts

Kendwa

Full moon beach parties, swimming at all tides, relaxed resort atmosphere.
Quiet & Remote

Matemwe & Kiwengwa

Quieter north-east beaches, reef snorkelling, boutique resorts, sunrise views.
Nature

Spice Farms & Inland

Famous spice farms, Jozani Forest, red colobus monkeys, rural village culture.
