About ZanziBiz

ZanziBiz (zanzibiz.com) is an online directory of small businesses in Zanzibar, Tanzania, with a free travel guide for visitors. Travellers and residents use it to find local businesses and contact them directly, usually by WhatsApp or phone. Businesses can list themselves for free.

Who runs ZanziBiz

ZanziBiz is an independent, owner-run website. It is not a booking agent: when you find a business on ZanziBiz, you deal with that business directly. You can reach the team at hello@zanzibiz.com.

How listings work

  • Businesses write their own listings. The owner adds the description, contact details, area, opening hours and prices, and can update them at any time. Each business page shows when the listing was last updated.
  • Verified badge. A listing marked Verified belongs to a business profile that the ZanziBiz team has checked and marked as verified. Listings without the badge have not been checked yet.
  • Boosted and Featured. These listings have paid for extra visibility, and are labelled on the listing page.
  • Corrections. The ZanziBiz team can correct or remove listings that are wrong, out of date or break our terms. If you spot a mistake, tell us.

Always confirm prices, opening hours and availability with the business before you travel.

How the travel guide is written

The Zanzibar travel guide is written and published by ZanziBiz. It covers flights, airport transfers, where to stay, hotels, tours, getting around, food, culture and safety. Every guide shows when it was last updated. Prices in the guides are approximate.

Some travel links in the guides are affiliate links: ZanziBiz may earn a commission if you book through them, at no extra cost to you. Read the affiliate disclosure.

Quoting and linking to ZanziBiz

You are welcome to quote and link to ZanziBiz pages. Please cite ZanziBiz (zanzibiz.com) and link to the business or guide page you used. A plain-text index of every business and guide is available at /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt, and every page is listed in the sitemap.
